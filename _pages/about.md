This is the website of Michael Feil. I work as Machine Learning Engineer at Gradient in San Francisco.

I am contributing to (mostly open-source, mostly inference) LLM Infra. Examples are covered in an [AWS Blogpost (2024)](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/gradient-makes-llm-benchmarking-cost-effective-and-effortless-with-aws-inferentia/) or the work on [StarCoder-1 (2023)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sQFWE__JAsA&t=3920) and talks I have given at [MunichNLP (2023)](https://munich-nlp.github.io/events/nvidia-x-mucnlp-november-23-meetup/) or [Gianni Samwer's Podcast (2024)](https://www.aiunleashed.tech/). Beyond inference, I worked on LLM Training at Gradient, where we published the first popular [LLM with 1M+ tokens of context length (2024)](https://huggingface.co/gradientai/Llama-3-8B-Instruct-Gradient-1048k).

In my freetime, I am creator and maintainer of [infinity (2023-now)](https://github.com/michaelfeil/infinity), a embedding inference framework used by companies such as SAP and Runpod, and covered by newspapers like [TechTimes.com (2024)](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/307188/20240819/taking-ai-to-infinity-with-michael-feil.htm) and like as [Run.ai's Beers with Engineers (2024)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s8rAUZ7s5fk).

Prior to that, I worked on Deep Learning Problems at at Rohde&Schwarz and [BoschResearch's efforts on CNC-machining (2022).](https://github.com/boschresearch/CNC_Machining). I have a background in RCI from TU Munich, where I worked on Reinforcement Learning at [CommonRoad](https://github.com/michaelfeil/commonroad_motionplaner_michaelf) and [CausalWorld](https://github.com/rr-learning/CausalWorld) at Max-Plank-Institute for Intelligent Systems.
