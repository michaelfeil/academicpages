This is the website of Michael Feil. I work as a Machine Learning Engineer at [Baseten.co](https://basten.co) in San Francisco - hiring (send me a email if you work on the intersection of ml inference, cuda kernels & LLMs).

I contribute to (mostly open-source, mostly inference) LLM Infra. Examples of my work are covered in an [AWS Blogpost (2024)](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/gradient-makes-llm-benchmarking-cost-effective-and-effortless-with-aws-inferentia/) or in the projects like [StarCoder-1 (2023)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sQFWE__JAsA&t=3920) and talks I've given at [MunichNLP (2023)](https://munich-nlp.github.io/events/nvidia-x-mucnlp-november-23-meetup/) or [Gianni Samwer's Podcast (2024)](https://www.aiunleashed.tech/). Beyond inference, I worked on LLM training at Gradient.ai, where we published the first popular [LLM with 1M+ tokens of context length (2024)](https://huggingface.co/gradientai/Llama-3-8B-Instruct-Gradient-1048k) which was highest ranking LLM on Huggingface, covered by us at [SigGraph'24](https://crusoe.ai/pdfs/SIGGRAPH+Crusoe+Gradient%20(Web).pdf) & [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/how-gradient-created-an-open-llm-with-a-million-token-context-window/), and used by great researchers at [Qwen](https://qwenlm.github.io/blog/qwen2.5-turbo/) & [MIT Han Lab](https://github.com/mit-han-lab/duo-attention).

In my free time, I am the creator and maintainer of [infinity (2023-now)](https://github.com/michaelfeil/infinity), an embedding inference framework used by companies such as [SAP](https://community.sap.com/t5/artificial-intelligence-and-machine-learning-blogs/bring-open-source-llms-into-sap-ai-core/ba-p/13655167), [Runpod](https://github.com/runpod-workers/worker-infinity-embedding), [Vast](https://vast.ai/article/serving-infinity) and covered by newspapers like [TechTimes.com (2024)](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/307188/20240819/taking-ai-to-infinity-with-michael-feil.htm) and featured in [Run.ai's Beers with Engineers (2024)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s8rAUZ7s5fk).

Before this, I worked on Deep Learning Problems at Rohde & Schwarz and [Bosch Research's efforts on CNC-machining (2022)](https://github.com/boschresearch/CNC_Machining). I have a background in RCI from TU Munich, where I worked on Frameworks for Reinforcement Learning at [CommonRoad](https://github.com/michaelfeil/commonroad_motionplaner_michaelf) and [CausalWorld](https://github.com/rr-learning/CausalWorld) at the Max-Planck-Institute for Intelligent Systems. I also experimented with [Blogging](https://michaelfeil.eu/academicpages/projects/projects-995-blog-fedavg-vs-fedpa-gd/) on Deep Learning Optimizers for Federated Learning.

If my GitHub commit graph is not green, or you have questions to ask, try to book a 90-minute session with me on Calendly at [Dogpatch Boulders](https://maps.app.goo.gl/VkhzLCwG9EZjcQh27).
